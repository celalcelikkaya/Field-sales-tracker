Products & prices

Audit trail

Every sale, edit, cancellation, payment and customer change is written to an append-only ledger. Each entry carries the fingerprint of the one before it, so any later change to the records can be detected.

Reset data

Deletes everything that was entered: sales, payments, customers, unpaid sales and the audit trail. Company information and your trial / licence are never changed. This cannot be undone.
